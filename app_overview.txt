================================================================================
                           STUDY TRACKER APPLICATION
                    Features, Benefits, and Future Roadmap
================================================================================

Study Tracker is a comprehensive, full-stack application designed to help
students organize, plan, track, and enhance their self-paced learning journeys.
Below is a detailed breakdown of the application's current features, its core
benefits, and proposed future improvements.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CURRENT APP FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   User Authentication & Secure Access:
    - Dedicated student profiles with secure login and signup features.
    - Personalizes the experience, keeping each student's subjects, plans, and
      notes isolated and private.

*   Subject Management:
    - Creation, modification, deletion, and management of various subjects.
    - Centralized dashboard detailing overall completion rates and status of
      all active courses.

*   Flexible YouTube Import (Playlists, Videos & Multi-links):
    - Direct integration with the YouTube Data API v3.
    - Allows students to import entire playlists, single videos, YouTube shorts, or shared links by entering their URLs or IDs.
    - Supports batch importing multiple links/IDs at once (separated by commas, spaces, or newlines).
    - Automatically extracts video metadata (titles, durations, sequence order) and populates the subject's lecture list.

*   Lecture & Progress Tracking:
    - Granular, interactive lecture checklists for each subject.
    - Toggle individual lectures or perform batch actions to mark videos as completed.
    - Real-time progress bars indicating percentage completion.

*   Smart Adaptive Study Planner:
    - Generates personalized study schedules based on daily available study hours or target completion deadlines.
    - Automatically distributes YouTube lectures/videos into balanced daily blocks.
    - Chronological Completed/Uncompleted Sorting: Backdates plans to the subject creation date, keeping completed lectures in the past, and scheduling remaining uncompleted lectures starting from today.
    - Dynamic "Adjust Plan" feature: Allows students to recalculate schedules, adapting the remaining study plan starting today if they get ahead of or fall behind their targets.

*   Real-time UI State Synchronization:
    - All dashboard progress counters, study planner assignments, and progress chart stats synchronize and update instantly in the UI upon checklist toggling or playlist importing without requiring manual page reloads.

*   Daily Goals (Micro-Tasking To-Do List):
    - Integrated daily goal manager (to-do list) directly on the interface.
    - Allows quick adding, editing, completing, and deleting of task items
      to establish focused daily routines.

*   AI Study Assistant (Gemini / LLM Integration):
    - AI Notes Generator: Instantly creates structured study notes, summaries,
      or cheat sheets for subjects.
    - AI Subject Chat: An interactive chat interface where students can ask the
      AI questions about the subject matter and get explanations or concept
      clarifications without leaving the app.

*   Visual Analytics & Completion Prediction:
    - Rich visualizations powered by Recharts (line/bar charts) to track historical
      progress trends.
    - Provides completion predictions and timelines based on historical velocity.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW WILL IT BENEFIT THE USER?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   Centralizes Learning Material:
    - Consolidates scattered YouTube tutorials, playlists, and coursework into one
      structured workspace. No more losing track of which video to watch next.

*   Removes Scheduling Overhead:
    - Eliminates the manual effort of calculating schedules. Students input their
      available hours, and the app builds a realistic daily plan, saving time
      and reducing mental fatigue.

*   Adaptive Time Management:
    - Keeps students motivated even when schedules break. If a student misses
      a study day, the adaptive plan adjustments redistribute the load gracefully,
      preventing burnout or feeling overwhelmed.

*   Enhances Retention and Understanding:
    - The AI Assistant acts as a 24/7 personal tutor. Students can immediately clear
      doubts or summarize lectures, leading to deeper comprehension and active learning.

*   Provides Motivational Feedback Loops:
    - Seeing visual progress charts, rising completion percentages, and ticked-off
      daily goals releases dopamine, boosting consistency and self-discipline.

*   Encourages Daily Execution:
    - By breaking down a massive curriculum into bite-sized daily goals, it turns
      daunting workloads into simple, executable daily tasks.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW CAN WE IMPROVE THE APP FURTHER?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   Embedded YouTube Video Player:
    - Integrate the YouTube player inside the web application so students can watch
      their lectures directly inside Study Tracker, with features like automatic progress
      syncing (e.g., auto-completing the lecture when the video finishes).
    - Provide a split-screen interface where students can watch the video while taking
      notes or chatting with the AI Assistant.

*   AI-Generated Quizzes and Flashcards:
    - Leverage the AI engine to generate multiple-choice questions, quizzes, and
      flashcards based on the imported lectures and notes.
    - Integrate Spaced Repetition Systems (SRS) (like Anki) to prompt reviews of
      concepts just as they are about to be forgotten.

*   In-App Focus / Pomodoro Timer:
    - Incorporate a Pomodoro timer or simple stopwatch directly within the subject dashboard.
    - Track the actual study hours spent vs. estimated study hours to provide deep
      productivity statistics and focus trends.

*   Multi-Source Integrations:
    - Extend import capabilities to support other learning sources: Coursera, Udemy,
      local PDF files, slide decks, and web articles.
    - Support OCR or document parsing to auto-summarize slide notes and textbooks.

*   Social Learning & Accountability (Gamification):
    - Introduce streaks, level-ups, experience points (XP), and badges to reward
      study habits.
    - Allow users to share public study plans or collaborate in "Study Rooms" with friends.

*   Progressive Web App (PWA) / Mobile Support:
    - Transform the front-end into a mobile-friendly PWA.
    - Implement local push notifications to remind students of their daily goals
      or alert them when they are falling behind their schedule.
